Einebenenmast  .... mast with horizontal conductor placement (1sys,2sys)
Tonnenmast ........ mast with ton-form conductor placement (2sys)
Donaumast ......... mast with triangle form conductor placement (2sys)
Reversed Donaumast  mast with reversed triangle form conductor placement (2sys)
Doppeltonnenmast .. mast with double tons conductor placement (4sys)
Vertikalmast ...... mast with vertical conductor placement (2sys left and right) 
Portalmast ........ mast with horizontal conductor placement (no ground,always only one sys, directly at substation )
Kombimast ......... mast with horizontal conductor placement below 
                     and with triangle conductor placement above (4sys,6sys)

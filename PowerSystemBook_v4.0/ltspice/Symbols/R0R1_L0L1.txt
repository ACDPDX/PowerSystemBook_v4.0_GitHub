* where there is a zero-system present
* All model parameters are the same for line.lib as for line2.lib
* But L0 and R0 the zero-system impedances are only guessed
* For cables:
* R0R1=2 L0L1=1.1

* 110kv R0R1=2.5 L0L1=3.5
* 220KV R0R1=2.5 L0L1=3
* 400KV R0R1=2.25 L0L1=2.5
* 750KV R0R1=2.0 L0L1=2.25
* 1150KV R0R1=1.75 L0L1=2.0
